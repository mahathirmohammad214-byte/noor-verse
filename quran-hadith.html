📖 Pobitra Al-Kuran

Al-Kuran mohan Allah-er bani ebong manusher jonner sothik pothnirdesh. Er orto bujhe o shikkha jibon e onushoron kora prottek musolmaner jonno zruri.

ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ

Bangla Orto:

"Eti shei kitab, jate kono shondeho nei; muttaki-der jonno pothnirdesh."

-- Sura Al-Bakra (2:2)

📜 Sahih Hadis (Niyot)

Rasulullah (ﷺ) bolechen:

إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

Bangla Orto:

"Nischoy-i sokol kajer folafol niyoter upor nirvorshil."

-- Sutro: Sahih Al-Bukhari, Hadis 1

💡 Kuran Shikhar Fozilot

Rasulullah (ﷺ) aro bolechen:

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

Bangla Orto:

"Tomader modhye shorbottom she bekti, je Kuran shekhe ebong onnoke shekhay."

-- Sutro: Sahih Al-Bukhari, Hadis 5027
